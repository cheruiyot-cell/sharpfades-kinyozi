Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Appointments

All appointments at SharpFades Nairobi are scheduled by booking via WhatsApp or phone. We prioritize appointments but we also accommodate walk-ins to ensure every client receives our full attention without delays.

2. Cancellations

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please notify us at least 4 hours in advance by WhatsApp (0702 555 093) or phone. This allows us to offer your slot to another client.

3. Late Arrivals

We hold your appointment slot for 10 minutes past the scheduled time. If you arrive later than that, we may need to reschedule your booking to accommodate other clients and avoid disrupting our schedule.

4. No-Show Policy

If you miss your appointment without notice (a "no-show"), we may implement the following:

  • First occurrence: A reminder notification sent via WhatsApp.
  • Second occurrence: You may be asked to provide a booking deposit (KES 50) to secure future appointments, which will be deducted from your final bill.
  • Repeated no-shows: We reserve the right to decline future bookings.

We understand that emergencies happen. If you encounter an unexpected situation, please contact us as soon as possible and we will do our best to accommodate you.

5. Refunds

We do not issue refunds for completed services. However, if you are not satisfied with your haircut, please take advantage of our 7-Day Fix Guarantee – return within 7 days and we will correct the service at no charge.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cancellation Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
