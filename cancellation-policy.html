Cancellation Policy: Simple, Fair, and Built Around You

Last updated: September 2026

Trusted by 500+ Nairobi clients · 4.9★ on Google · 7-Day Fix Guarantee on every cut

We're not here to trap you. We're here to give you the sharpest cut in Nairobi — and that means treating you like a human, not a contract.

Here's how we handle appointments, cancellations, and everything in between.

The short version

  • Booking: One WhatsApp message. Confirmed in under 15 minutes during business hours.
  • Need to cancel or reschedule? Give us 4 hours' notice on WhatsApp. No fees, no guilt.
  • Running late? Message us. We hold your slot for 10 minutes.
  • Missed an appointment? First time is free. We only ask for a deposit if it becomes a pattern.
  • Not happy with your cut? Come back within 7 days and we fix it free.

1. Booking Your Slot

Book by WhatsApp or phone — no apps to download, no accounts to create. We'll confirm your slot, tell you exactly what to expect, and hold your spot. Walk-ins are always welcome too, but booking guarantees zero waiting.

The benefit: a confirmed time, a confirmed price, and a confirmed style. No surprises.

2. Need to Cancel or Reschedule?

Just give us 4 hours' notice on WhatsApp (0702 555 093) or by phone. That's it. No fees, no guilt, no awkwardness.

Why 4 hours? It gives us time to offer your slot to someone else who needs it — which is exactly how we'd want to be treated if we were in your shoes.

3. Running Late?

Send us a quick message on WhatsApp. We hold your slot for 10 minutes past your scheduled time. After that, we'll do our best to fit you in — but we may need to rebook you to keep things fair for the next client.

Nairobi traffic is real. We get it. Just tell us.

4. If You Miss an Appointment Without Telling Us

We call this a no-show — but we don't treat you like a criminal for it.

  • First time: No stress. We'll send a friendly WhatsApp to check in.
  • Second time: We'll ask for a small KES 50 deposit to hold your next slot. It comes right off your final bill.
  • If it keeps happening: We may not be the right fit — but we'll always be honest with you about it.

Emergencies? Just tell us. We're human. We'll work with you.

5. Not Happy With Your Cut?

That's on us. Come back within 7 days and we'll fix it — free. No questions asked. That's the 7-Day Fix Guarantee, and it applies to every single cut.

We don't issue refunds for completed services, but we will always make it right.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
