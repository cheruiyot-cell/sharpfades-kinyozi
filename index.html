Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book? Do you accept walk-ins?

Tap any "Book on WhatsApp" button. I confirm within 15 minutes during business hours. Walk-ins are welcome too — booking guarantees zero waiting.

What if I'm running late?

Message me on WhatsApp immediately. I hold your slot for 10 minutes past your booked time. After that, I may need to reschedule to be fair to the next client.

Can I send a reference photo?

Absolutely. Send it on WhatsApp before your appointment and we'll discuss how to achieve the look.

What are your prices and payment methods?

Prices start at KES 200 for a basic cut. I'll confirm the final price before we start. I accept M-Pesa, Cash, and Card.

I've had bad barber experiences before. What's different?

Send me a photo of what you want. I'll tell you if it's doable. If it's not, I'll say so. If you're still not happy, come back in 7 days and I'll fix it free. You risk nothing.

Do you only do fades?

No. Cuts, beards, facials, dreads, colors. If it grows, I handle it.

Will you pressure me to buy add-ons?

Never. Bestsellers are packages, but you choose. No pressure, ever.

Where are you located and is parking easy?

Kilimani Mall, Tigoni Road, Nairobi. Free parking available inside the mall. See the Contact section for directions.
