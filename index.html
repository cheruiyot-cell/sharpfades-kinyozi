Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book? Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are always welcome! However, booking via WhatsApp or phone guarantees your spot and zero waiting. We recommend booking to secure your preferred time.

What if I'm running late?

Please WhatsApp us immediately. We hold your slot for 10 minutes past your booked time. After that, we may need to reschedule to be fair to the next client.

Can I send a reference photo?

Absolutely! Send it on WhatsApp before your appointment, and we'll discuss how to achieve the look.

What are your prices and payment methods?

Prices start at KES 200 for a basic cut. We'll confirm the final price before we start. We accept M-Pesa, Cash, and Card.

I've had bad barber experiences before. What's different?

Send us a photo of what you want. We'll tell you if it's doable. If it's not, we'll say so. If you're still not happy, come back in 7 days and we'll fix it free. You risk nothing.

Do you only do fades?

No. Cuts, beards, facials, dreads, colors. If it grows, we handle it.

Will you pressure me to buy add-ons?

We never upsell. Our bestsellers are packages, but you choose. No pressure, ever.

Where are you located and is parking easy?

We're in Kilimani Mall, Tigoni Road, Nairobi. Free parking available inside the mall. See the Contact section for directions.
