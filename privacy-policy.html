Privacy Policy: Plain English, No Fine Print

Last updated: September 2026

Trusted by 500+ Nairobi clients · 4.9★ on Google · We never sell your data

Your privacy matters. This page explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how you stay in control. No legalese, no tricks.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to book your appointment and give you a great cut.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information. Ever.
  • We never store card details — payments happen in person via M-Pesa, cash, or card.
  • You can ask us to delete your data anytime. One WhatsApp message is enough.
  • We only message you about your booking — unless you've asked us for offers.

1. Who We Are

SharpFades Nairobi ("we", "our", "us") operates sharpsfades.co.ke and provides barbering services from Kilimani Mall, Tigoni Road, Nairobi. If you have any privacy question, you can reach a real person — details at the bottom of this page.

2. What We Collect

Only what's needed to serve you well:

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, and anything else you share when booking on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • Booking details: your preferred service, appointment time, and any reference photos you send us.
  • Payment: we do not collect or store payment details on this website. Payments are handled in person via M-Pesa, cash, or card.
  • Technical data: basic browser and device information, plus cookies, if you browse the site.

3. How We Use It

  • To confirm and manage your booking.
  • To message you about your appointment — changes, reminders, follow-ups.
  • To improve our website and the service you get in the chair.
  • To send you offers — only if you've opted in. You can opt out any time.

4. What We Never Do

  • We never sell or rent your personal information.
  • We never spam you with marketing you didn't ask for.
  • We never store your card or M-Pesa credentials.
  • We never share your reference photos publicly without asking.

5. Who We Share With

Only where strictly necessary:

  • Trusted service providers who help us run the business (for example, payment processing or hosting).
  • Legal authorities, if the law requires it.

That's the complete list.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Access is limited to the people who need it to serve you.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to make browsing smoother and to understand which pages are useful. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings — though a few features may stop working properly.

8. Your Rights

You're in control. You can:

  • Ask to see the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Opt out of marketing at any time.

To do any of these, message us on WhatsApp or call 0702 555 093. We'll action it promptly.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site links to platforms like Instagram and Facebook. Their privacy practices are their own — we're not responsible for how they handle your data.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. Kids' cuts are booked by a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a revised date. No silent changes.
